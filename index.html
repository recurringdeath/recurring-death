The Band

RECURRING DEATH is a death metal band from Greece, forged in darkness and driven by brutality.


The band's sound is built around crushing riffs, relentless aggression and a dark atmosphere, creating a raw death metal experience.


Their upcoming album "Spiritual Equilibrium" contains 10 tracks and marks the band's next chapter.
